# Chord Expert – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

Chord Expert does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No internet access.** The app works completely offline and never sends data anywhere.
- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.**
- **Microphone.** The tuner listens to the microphone only while the tuner is open, to detect the pitch of your instrument. The sound is processed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored or transmitted.
- **Settings and favourites** are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.
